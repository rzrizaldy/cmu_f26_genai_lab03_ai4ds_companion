When do we raise a flag?

We raise a flag when at least 20 reviews came in and the 1–2 star share hits our line. A flag means “go read the reviews”, not “we found the cause”.

Data quality and limits

    We read review times as UTC. The reminder logs, the social captions, and these reviews come from separate public sources and different people. What connects them is our workflow, not a single learner’s journey.
